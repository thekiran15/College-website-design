Our Courses

Courses We Offer

At CIT College, we offer a wide range of programs from PUC to Postgraduate levels. Our courses are designed
to meet modern industry standards with a strong academic foundation. Whether you're stepping into college
or advancing your career, we have the right program for you.

Intermediate

Kickstart your academic journey with our Pre-University (PUC) programs designed for aspiring minds. We offer strong foundations in Science and Commerce streams with expert faculty support. The curriculum blends theoretical concepts with practical learning and lab experience. Special coaching for competitive exams like CET, NEET, and JEE is integrated. Step confidently into your future with a balanced, career-oriented education.

Degree

Our undergraduate programs in engineering and management build core technical and leadership skills. Choose from disciplines like Computer Science, Electronics, Mechanical, Civil, and more. Industry-relevant curriculum, hands-on labs, and internship opportunities prepare you for real-world challenges. With dedicated placement support and career guidance, students secure top jobs globally. Join us to become an innovative, socially responsible, and globally competent graduate.

Post Graduation

Take your education to the next level with our cutting-edge postgraduate programs. We offer M.Tech and MBA courses that blend advanced knowledge with research and industry exposure. Live projects, seminars, and workshops enhance professional skills and critical thinking. Our faculty includes Ph.D. scholars, researchers, and corporate experts guiding your journey. Elevate your career with specialized, future-ready postgraduate education at CIT.

Our Facilities

CIT College provides a well-equipped campus with modern classrooms, advanced labs, and a vast digital library.
Students enjoy comfortable hostels, sports grounds, cafeterias, and Wi-Fi-enabled learning spaces.
Every facility is designed to support academic excellence and all-round student development.

Library

Our spacious library is stocked with thousands of books, journals, and digital resources. It offers a quiet and comfortable environment, perfect for focused study and research.

Play Ground

CIT's lush green playground encourages students to stay active and healthy. We support various sports like cricket, football, volleyball, and athletics to promote team spirit and fitness.

Tasty and Healthy Food

Our hygienic canteen serves fresh, nutritious, and tasty meals every day. We prioritize both health and flavor, offering a balanced diet to fuel students throughout the day.

About Us

Channabasaveshwara Institute of Technology (CIT), Gubbi, is dedicated to delivering quality technical and professional education.
Established in 2001, our mission is to empower rural and urban students with world-class knowledge and skills.
