About Us

We are the Tumkur's largest college

Channabasaveshwara Institute of Technology (CIT), Gubbi, proudly holds the distinction of being Tumkur’s largest and most comprehensive engineering college. With a sprawling campus spread over 60 acres of lush greenery, world-class infrastructure, and a diverse range of academic programs, CIT has become a leading hub for technical and professional education in the region. Since its establishment in 2001, the institution has consistently grown in size, quality, and reputation, attracting students from across Karnataka and beyond.
